Receiver

DeepSpaceTelemetry.Receiver — Module
Receiver

The ground-station loop: bandwidth-paced ingestion with stochastic loss and retransmission, the retention custodian, and the metrics and events_rx.csv recording. Re-entrant: a restarted receiver reseeds retry and custodial state from the event log. The derived products live in Masks and MissionFigures.

DeepSpaceTelemetry.Receiver.ServicePacer — Type
ServicePacer

The running completion instant sim of the receiver's download slot on the mission timeline of the TelemetryCore.TickClock. Consecutive slots are chained against it (serve_slot!), so back-to-back transfers follow one another at the modeled rate.

DeepSpaceTelemetry.Receiver.serve_slot! — Function
serve_slot!(pacer::ServicePacer, clock::TelemetryCore.TickClock, slot_sim_sec::Real)

Waits out one download slot of slot_sim_sec mission seconds. The slot starts when the previous one completed, or now after an idle stretch of at least one slot, and the task parks on the tick clock until it ends (TelemetryCore.wait_until!), so the slot elapses as mission time exactly.

DeepSpaceTelemetry.Receiver.run_receiver — Function
run_receiver(clock, link, run_id; ...)

The ground-station loop: serves the batches on link/, each transfer taking batch_transfer_sec divided by the effective link capacity (visibility profile × disruption factor), draws one loss realization from loss_model per transfer attempt, and moves delivered batches to ground/ and retry-exhausted ones to lost/. Every milestone is appended to events_rx.csv, from which the post-processing reconstructs the run exactly.

clock is the TelemetryCore.TickClock of the mission, paced or not: in wall mode the driver (TelemetryCore.run_ticks!) holds each wakeup back to its wall instant, which changes when the loop runs but not what it records. Every wait parks the task on the clock, and the loop ends at the horizon (TelemetryCore.horizon_reached), on the stop flag, or on the HALT sentinel.

A lost transfer leaves the batch on the link; its retransmission is served no earlier than round_trip_light_time_sec after the loss was detected (deferred negative acknowledgment), while the other in-flight batches keep being served. A batch whose transfer fails max_retries + 1 times moves to lost/, data preserved, which frees its slot in the emitter's transmission window (in-flight occupancy is the link/ listing). A re-attaching receiver reseeds retry and custodial state from the event log and synthesizes ingested records, at the re-attach instant, for batches in ground/ whose record a crash between the delivery move and the log append lost.

With retention.enabled the loop also runs the retention custodian: while the delivered-payload tally exceeds watermark_bytes, the oldest-ingested batches, once at least grace of mission time past their ingestion, lose their seg_*.csv payload files, keep metadata.json, gain a PRUNED marker, and are recorded as pruned in events_rx.csv. Event logs, metrics, masks, and lost/ are not pruned. The pruning queue is materialized on watermark breach (delivered_payload_queue).

Keyword arguments

  • orig_stdout: stream receiving the console status panel.
  • status_panel: render the clear-screen console status panel (mission day, link state, ground and lost tallies) to orig_stdout on every loop iteration; off by default, set by the supervisor from dashboard.receiver_status_panel.
  • batch_transfer_sec: transfer time of one batch at full link capacity [mission s] (telemetry_settings(cfg).nominal_batch_transfer_sec).
  • loss_model: stochastic packet-loss channel (ChannelEffects.LossModel).
  • max_retries: retransmissions allowed per batch; 0 moves a batch to lost/ at its first failed transfer.
  • retention: the custodian's TelemetryCore.RetentionPolicy.
  • stop: cooperative stop flag raised by the supervisor.
  • min_link_factor: capacity floor below which no transfer is attempted.
  • round_trip_light_time_sec: earliest retransmission delay after a detected loss [mission s]; 0.0 retries immediately.
  • hole_feed: the declared-hole feed the receiver appends its lost and pruned batches to, nothing for none.

DeepSpaceTelemetry.Receiver.delivered_payload_queue — Function
delivered_payload_queue(run_dir::String, ground_path::String)
    -> Vector{Tuple{DateTime,String,Int}}

Materializes the retention custodian's pruning queue from the run's ground census and events_rx.csv: delivered (ingested) batches whose payload has not been pruned, oldest-ingested first, each with its current seg_*.csv payload size in bytes. Consulted at startup for the payload tally and lazily on watermark breach, so the in-memory queue stays empty outside breach episodes.
