DeepSpaceTelemetryHeadless.Receiver — Module
Receiver

The ground-station loop: bandwidth-paced ingestion with stochastic loss and retransmission, the retention custodian, and the metrics and events_rx.csv recording. Re-entrant: a restarted receiver reseeds retry and custodial state from the event log. The derived products live in Masks and MissionFigures.

DeepSpaceTelemetryHeadless.Receiver.run_receiver — Function
run_receiver(clock, link, run_dir; ...)

The ground-station loop: serves the batches on <run_dir>/link/, each transfer lasting until the effective link capacity (visibility profile × disruption factor) integrated from its start reaches batch_transfer_sec (ChannelEffects.transfer_completion), so that a transfer interrupted by the end of a contact or a blackout resumes at the next contact; it draws one loss realization from loss_model per transfer attempt, with the largest loss multiplier of the events active during the transfer, and records the outcome at the completion instant. A transfer that would complete at or after the horizon has no outcome. The loop moves delivered batches to ground/ and retry-exhausted ones to lost/. Every milestone is appended to events_rx.csv, from which the post-processing reconstructs the run exactly. run_dir is the absolute path of the run directory.

clock is the TelemetryCore.TickClock of the mission; every wait parks the task on it. The loop ends at the horizon, on the stop flag, or on the HALT sentinel (TelemetryCore.loop_should_stop).

A lost transfer leaves the batch on the link; its retransmission is served no earlier than round_trip_light_time_sec after the loss was detected (deferred negative acknowledgment), while the other in-flight batches keep being served. A batch whose transfer fails max_retries + 1 times moves to lost/, data preserved, which frees its slot in the emitter's transmission window (in-flight occupancy is the link/ listing). A re-attaching receiver reseeds retry and custodial state from the event log (reattach_state).

With retention.enabled the loop also runs the retention custodian (custodian_step!): while the delivered-payload tally exceeds watermark_bytes, the oldest-ingested batches, once at least grace of mission time past their ingestion, lose their seg_*.csv payload files, keep metadata.json, gain a PRUNED marker, and are recorded as pruned in events_rx.csv. Event logs, metrics, masks, and lost/ are not pruned.

Keyword arguments

  • orig_stdout: stream receiving the console status panel.
  • status_panel: render the clear-screen console status panel (status_panel_text) to orig_stdout on every loop iteration; off by default, set by the supervisor from dashboard.receiver_status_panel.
  • batch_transfer_sec: transfer time of one batch at full link capacity [mission s] (telemetry_settings(cfg).nominal_batch_transfer_sec); at a lower capacity the transfer lasts until the integrated capacity reaches it.
  • loss_model: stochastic packet-loss channel (ChannelEffects.LossModel).
  • max_retries: retransmissions allowed per batch; 0 moves a batch to lost/ at its first failed transfer.
  • retention: the custodian's TelemetryCore.RetentionPolicy.
  • stop: cooperative stop flag raised by the supervisor.
  • min_link_factor: capacity floor below which no transfer is attempted.
  • round_trip_light_time_sec: earliest retransmission delay after the completion of a failed transfer [mission s]; 0.0 retries immediately.
  • hole_feed: the declared-hole feed the receiver appends its lost and pruned batches to, nothing for none.
  • onboard_count: the emitter's count of batches on board (Emitter.run_emitter), read for the metrics; nothing lists onboard/ on every iteration instead.

DeepSpaceTelemetryHeadless.Receiver.reattach_state — Function
reattach_state(run_dir, link_path, ground_names, clock, retention) -> NamedTuple

Retry and custodial state of a receiver attaching to a run, from its events_rx.csv: retry_counts, the failed attempts of each batch still on the link; total_retries, the run's retry rows; and payload_bytes, the delivered-payload tally (zero unless retention.enabled). A restarted receiver must not grant fresh retry budgets to in-flight batches nor forget custodial state. Batches of ground_names without an ingested record witness a crash between the delivery move and the log append; their record is synthesized at the current mission instant, as the delivery time is unrecoverable, so masks, custodian, and consumers agree the batch is delivered. A run without events yields the empty state.

DeepSpaceTelemetryHeadless.Receiver.RetentionCustodian — Type
RetentionCustodian

State of the retention custodian: queue, the delivered batches as (ingest instant, name, payload bytes), oldest-ingested first, materialized on watermark breach (delivered_payload_queue) and empty between breach episodes; and payload_bytes, the running tally of prunable payload compared with the watermark.

DeepSpaceTelemetryHeadless.Receiver.custodian_step! — Function
custodian_step!(custodian, retention, run_dir, sim_t; hole_feed = nothing)

One pass of the retention custodian at sim_t: while the tally exceeds retention.watermark_bytes, the oldest-ingested batch at least retention.grace past its ingestion loses its payload. Each prune appends the pruned event row and declares the hole first, then writes the PRUNED marker, then deletes the payload, so a consumer never sees a payload-less batch without its pruned row. An I/O failure is logged and leaves the tally unchanged, as the files are still on disk; the batch is not re-queued.

DeepSpaceTelemetryHeadless.Receiver.delivered_payload_queue — Function
delivered_payload_queue(run_dir::String, ground_path::String)
    -> Vector{Tuple{DateTime,String,Int}}

Materializes the retention custodian's pruning queue from the run's ground census and events_rx.csv: delivered (ingested) batches whose payload has not been pruned, oldest-ingested first, each with its current seg_*.csv payload size in bytes. Consulted at startup for the payload tally and lazily on watermark breach, so the in-memory queue stays empty outside breach episodes.
